## Finance and utilization: HCRIS versus PHC4

For a facility's reported finances or utilization, begin with [CMS Hospital 2552-10](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/statistics-trends-and-reports/cost-reports/hospital-2552-2010-form) (page sections “Hospital 2552-2010 form” and downloadable fiscal-year files). CMS describes cost-report data and links files. Select a specific fiscal year and report status, then review the applicable form, instructions and data dictionary before interpreting any field. Fiscal reporting periods may differ across hospitals and from calendar-year encounter counts. Medicare cost reporting does not establish complete coverage of all Pennsylvania facilities. Download availability on a landing page does not establish fitness for a particular question.

[PHC4 Available Data](https://www.phc4.org/request-custom-data/available-data/) sections “Inpatient Discharge Data” and “Standard and Custom Data” describe Pennsylvania hospital discharge records, with a stated Q1 1990–Q1 2026 availability at the time of review, and standard statewide, regional and facility files. Regional files describe facilities in the region, not necessarily residents of that region. The same page says supplemental facility files, when applicable, contain a bed count; its type and applicable period have not been established. Its financial products require separate product-specific review: discharge records and facility financial profiles have different grains and purposes. The [custom-data request page](https://www.phc4.org/request-custom-data/) describes a request route; price, eligibility, agreement, approval and release-specific delivery are unresolved. No request or purchase was made. For encounter counts, assess reporting coverage, diagnosis definitions, charge versus payment, and the exact period before comparison to HCRIS.

**Capacity and utilization gap:** Neither source's cited landing page establishes the exact HCRIS worksheet, line, column, unit, reporting population and fiscal period for a proposed bed, patient-day or discharge measure. Review the release-bound 2552-10 instructions before calling a measure licensed, certified or staffed beds. For PHC4, obtain the applicable facility-file definition and period through an authorized process. No cross-source equivalence is established.

## Ownership and affiliation

[CMS Hospital All Owners](https://data.cms.gov/provider-characteristics/hospitals-and-other-facilities/hospital-all-owners/api-docs) is a candidate for enrollment-related owner associations. The direct documentation response obtained here was only a JavaScript shell; it does not establish the fields, API version or current access. Before use, independently examine the specific release's [CMS guidance](https://data.cms.gov/sites/default/files/2022-12/0440fd0b-79fc-451e-8d75-73f3a5b7b0ab/Hospital_Data_Guidance.pdf) and dictionary, role and effective dates, person versus organization, historical scope, and enrollment identifiers. Ownership category, owner association and system affiliation are different concepts. A shared identifier or name alone does not establish a valid join.

[AHRQ Compendium](https://www.ahrq.gov/chsp/data-resources/compendium.html), sections “Data Files” and “Definitions of Health Systems,” describes separate system and hospital files covering a single calendar year and lists hospital files for 2016, 2018, 2020–2023. The page is useful for identifying a possible system-affiliation alternative, not proving ownership or an exact HCRIS crosswalk. The direct request returned an empty HTTP 202 response; the page content was independently visible through the web reader, but no locally captured bytes support a ledger assertion. Review the chosen year's documentation and identity/temporal mismatches before linking.

[Hospital General Information](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/dataset/xubh-q36u) is a potential facility-directory comparator; the direct request returned 403, so this attempt provides no field or access verification. Do not treat it as a licensed-bed source. Inspect a permitted release and dictionary before proposing identifiers or a population.

## Other workflow alternatives

[HCUP state database availability](https://hcup-us.ahrq.gov/db/availability_public.jsp) is a starting point for a discharge-based question. Verify the requested Pennsylvania product and year, application, training, agreement and cost from the current publisher instructions; an availability table is not access or permission to redistribute records. HCUP is not a hospital financial statement or ownership inventory.

[MEPS](https://meps.ahrq.gov/mepsweb/) may support a population and health-care-use question. Select the relevant survey component, year, sampling frame, weights and documentation; it does not replace hospital financial accounts. No file or respondent data was downloaded.

## Review and refresh

A human reviewer must check each proposed claim against the captured source and locator, decide rights for metadata, retention and reuse, and document decisions before assertions enter the accepted ledger. Verify current releases, periods, coverage, access and terms at each selection; refresh after publisher revisions or a change to the question. Resolve whether a requested analysis needs facility, hospital-system, owner-association, discharge or survey-person grain. Do not infer join compatibility, clinical validity, actual access or research fitness from this inventory.
