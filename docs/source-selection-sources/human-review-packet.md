## Decision worksheet and ledger handoff

For **each** proposed row, record: proposal ID; `accept`, `reject`, or `defer`; actual reviewer and role; UTC review timestamp; rationale; checked source URL and exact locator; evidence/capture ID; whether source still supports the proposed wording; and any replacement wording, scope or follow-up. Record a distinct decision for core membership and its rationale if requested; acceptance of a claim does not by itself designate a source core or decision-ready. A rejection or deferral should record why and what would change it. No reviewer has yet been assigned to a row.

For an accepted claim, a subsequent authorized ledger editor must create a unique `assertion_id` with `subject_id`, supported `predicate`, typed `value`, `scope`, `observed_at`, `evidence_refs` containing the matching asset's capture ID and locator, and the actual accepting `decision_id`. The matching decision needs a unique ID, `decision: "accept"`, reviewer, reviewer_role, rationale, UTC `reviewed_at`, subject_id, evidence_ids and assertion_ids. Review time cannot precede the evidence observation. The contract requires each assertion capture's `metadata_capture` right to be `allowed`; until a real rights decision supports that status, do not insert an accepted assertion. A `needs_capture` row first needs an appropriate successful capture linked to the same asset and rights record. Reject/defer decisions must not be represented as accepted assertions. Release-scoped dictionaries and joins additionally require actual release and distribution records and matching scoped captures; do not invent these identifiers. Validate the resulting ledger before publication.

Public reuse requires a separate explicit `publish_metadata: "allowed"` decision covering the projected metadata and citations in HTML, APIs and user exports. Permission to capture metadata, or permission for display alone, does not satisfy that gate. Raw bytes, quotations and dataset payloads retain their separate permissions. Public identity also needs an accepted, permitted identity assertion matching the asset ID, publisher ID and title. An asset without that identity is omitted from the public projection; its internal inventory record remains available for review.
