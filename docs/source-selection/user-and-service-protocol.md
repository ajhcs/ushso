# User and service evidence protocol

Status: prepared; no participant recruited, consent obtained, session observed, offer sent or payment received by this work.

Recruiting draft: “We are studying how health-services researchers choose data sources. We invite you to describe a recent non-sensitive source-selection task and try a catalog prototype. Participation is voluntary. We will ask about useful and unsuitable sources, access steps and unresolved questions. Please do not share patient, confidential employer or unpublished sensitive information.” Sending requires actual outreach authorization.

Consent draft: “You may stop at any time. With your consent, we will record task events and anonymous notes for product improvement. We will explain retention, access and recording arrangements before the session. No audio/video recording occurs without separate consent.” The operating owner must supply actual retention and contact arrangements before use.

For each session retain a private participant code, role, consent revision, eligible task, task-start time, first-useful-source time, shortlist-completion time, end time, critical mistakes, assistance, abandonment and observer. Keep identifying details and research questions outside the public repository. Public derived records must omit identifying details. Do not infer representativeness from roles.

A defensible shortlist requires correct identity, evidence or explicit uncertainty for measures/population/geography/time, access burden, limitations and an actionable acquisition step. A critical false assertion fails the task even if completed quickly. Record task completion, rubric success, confidence and elapsed time separately. Primary denominator includes every consented attempted eligible task, including abandonment and technical failure. Exclusions need a prospective rule and retained reason. Participant and reviewer time are separate quantities.

For a synthetic service rehearsal, create a private engagement record with scope version, quote assumptions, source evidence, producer/reviewer time, review disagreements, delivery artifact, access failure and cancellation disposition. Use no real customer identity. A completed rehearsal supports workflow verification only. Real engagements require explicit authorization, rights/consent and an independent review; any quote acceptance and actual payment remain separate events.

Track fee, refunded amount, hosting/API/payment costs, producer/reviewer/admin/support hours and owner-supplied time valuation. Contribution is net revenue minus attributable nonlabor cost and valued labor. Price-floor scenarios divide loaded cost by one minus target margin. Neither scenarios nor dry-run revenues are market evidence. Begin at one active engagement until actual reviewer capacity supports a change. Keep failed, cancelled, blocked and revised-scope attempts in operating records.

At 30/60/90 days after actual focused release, assess usability, burden, repeat tasks, payment evidence and observed exposure. Review at 90 days even with zero engagements. Zero exposure cannot establish lack of demand; a delivery alone cannot establish repeat use. No release date or business outcome has been observed here.
