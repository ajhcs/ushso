# Hospital CSV MRF formats

Policy version: `ushso.hospital-csv-mrf.v1`.

CMS hospital-price-transparency wide and tall CSV v3.0.0 official fictional examples are pinned. A generic CSV header parser cannot misclassify a metadata row as the variable schema.

Wide and tall synthetic representations yield equivalent selected charges without losing payer/plan associations. Format equivalence applies to the fixture values only; it does not prove two real hospital files describe identical periods/services.

Partial or malformed rows remain visible and cannot fabricate zero prices. Last-good generation remains `live-2026-09-03-85b50522b420`.
