# Price dimensions and sample quality

Policy version: `ushso.price-semantics.v1`.

A gross charge, discounted cash price, negotiated amount and allowed amount remain different measures. Algorithm, percentage and per-diem values remain non-comparable.

Missing or negative/zero values are investigated by documented semantics, not universally dropped or treated as valid prices. A passing sample does not imply legal compliance, complete hospital reporting or comparable patient costs. Current pinned CMS hospital v3.0.0 and payer TiC v2.2.1 definitions are used, not an obsolete generic MRF schema.
