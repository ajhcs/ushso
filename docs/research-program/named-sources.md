# Named sources and release families

Policy version: `ushso.named-sources.v1`.

Exact named-source requests distinguish the product family from related catalog text. Reviewed names, acronyms, and exact product anchors cover the core cohort and twelve expansion families. Not-yet-indexed identities are recorded without inventing catalog membership.

MEPS, HCUP, NPPES, and PHC4 exact-name fixtures cannot resolve to unrelated descriptions that merely mention similar words. Family relations come from source-native links and documented series. The 579 repeated-title cases are accounted for. Title equality alone never deletes or merges a record; ambiguous title matches remain candidates.

Named-source matches have their own result state. An unavailable named source returns a specific bounded coverage explanation, not a fabricated exact match. Broader context is labeled separately. Catalog membership and source-family recognition remain distinct.

Sealed `packages/identity/src/family-graph.mjs` and fingerprint-sealed `packages/retrieval/tools/question-parser.mjs` are not edited by this implementation.
