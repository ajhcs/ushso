## Atomic commits

### C-002-1 — Define cohort identities

Write source-native product anchors for the 100-product cohort across the ten research domains in the master plan. Store product/release distinction, selection rationale and public/restricted expectation; seed all 3434 baseline record IDs separately.

**Verify:** No duplicate products counted through annual releases; every anchor resolves to retained source evidence or a named intake task.

### C-002-2 — Freeze research tasks and negative cases

Create novice, advanced and machine tasks including HCRIS/PHC4, maternal versus infant mortality, ACS geography, nursing staffing, price definitions and forbidden joins. Mark holdout labels inaccessible to implementer tuning.

**Verify:** Hash cohort/tasks; every requirement R01–R16 has a measurable acceptance row with denominator and evidence owner.

### C-002-3 — Pin MRF and expansion scope

Choose 25 hospitals across states/ownership types and ten payer reporting entities using authoritative directories. Include the twelve named missing families and map each to a bounded source intake.

**Verify:** Selection is documented before endpoint results are seen; unavailable items stay in the denominator and cannot be silently replaced.
