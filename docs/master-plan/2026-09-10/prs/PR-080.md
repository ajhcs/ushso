## Atomic commits

### C-080-1 — Prepare a reproducible moderated protocol

Specify eight novice/eight advanced participants, frozen tasks, success criteria, help rules and non-PHI notes. Separate human participants from agent simulations.

**Verify:** Tasks test finding, interpreting, accessing and citing sources; merely clicking through a page is not completion.

### C-080-2 — Collect consented task evidence

Run the protocol or ingest documented sessions from the product owner/research facilitator. Record completion, critical misunderstandings, observed friction and untested cases.

**Verify:** If participants are unavailable, retain unverified status; implementer self-tests cannot satisfy R12.

### C-080-3 — Report and route concrete failures

Compute completion by group and task, with counts and limitations; create targeted fixes for access/grain/price misunderstandings and poor navigation.

**Verify:** Require ≥85% completion in each group and no critical misunderstanding; tiny-group results are product acceptance evidence, not a population-wide usability claim.
