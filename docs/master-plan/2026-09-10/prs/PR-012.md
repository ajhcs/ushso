## Atomic commits

### C-012-1 — Reserve source request budgets

Add per-host concurrency and daily request/byte reservations with UTC periods and a source-specific override table. Reuse the durable lease store.

**Verify:** Concurrent workers cannot both spend the last reservation; cancellation releases only unused reservations.

### C-012-2 — Classify and schedule retries

Retry documented transient timeouts/429/5xx with bounded attempts and Retry-After. Route credential, parser and scientific ambiguity to specific queues without blind retries.

**Verify:** Fixture traces show the exact maximum request count and delay schedule for each outcome.

### C-012-3 — Reconcile resumed source runs

Track completed, waiting, failed and skipped records with source-generation pins. Emit a denominator report after a restart or partial run.

**Verify:** Repeated completion/retry messages remain idempotent; a newer source capture cannot be overwritten by an older resumed job.
