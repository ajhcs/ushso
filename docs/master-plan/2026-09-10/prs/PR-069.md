## Atomic commits

### C-069-1 — Define citation formats

Generate plain-text, BibTeX/RIS and machine-readable source citations from publisher, product, release, canonical locator and observed/accessed dates. Mark absent DOI/license data honestly.

**Verify:** Do not invent authors, DOI, release year or publisher endorsement; output escaping preserves special characters.

### C-069-2 — Export the evidence packet

Include selected sources, contexts, fields, recipes, limitations and receipt links; let users explicitly choose whether to include their question/notes.

**Verify:** Exports exclude credentials and default to source evidence only; they validate against the versioned packet schema.

### C-069-3 — Verify re-import and citation fidelity

Open an exported packet in a clean client and resolve retained contexts or typed retired-generation states. Provide a readable summary alongside JSON.

**Verify:** The exported source order and generation agree with the UI; reproducing a selection does not silently re-run a different search.
