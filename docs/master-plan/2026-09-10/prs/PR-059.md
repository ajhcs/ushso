## Atomic commits

### C-059-1 — Unify cursor and generation guidance

Map expired, malformed and wrong-generation cursors to precise restart instructions that preserve the research question and disclose snapshot change.

**Verify:** A cursor for one tool/query cannot page a different collection; an expired cursor never silently begins a new generation.

### C-059-2 — Preserve typed domain results

Keep partial, unknown, empty, unavailable and blocked separate through HTTP/client envelopes. Align retryable flags and corrective actions.

**Verify:** An unknown schema is not returned as an empty list of variables or a transport success claiming the task completed.

### C-059-3 — Verify cancellation and output limits

Exercise cancellation, slow responses, bounded collection output and oversize handling through the real client transport fixtures.

**Verify:** Cancellation stops the current request; clients neither retry nonretryable states nor lose the next-cursor/omitted-section explanation.
