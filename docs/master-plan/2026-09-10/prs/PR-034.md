## Atomic commits

### C-034-1 — Define identifier-system metadata

Represent CCN, NPI, FIPS/GEOID, source report IDs, tax identifiers and plan identifiers with source-specific formatting and entity role. Preserve leading zeros.

**Verify:** CCN and NPI are never interchangeable; equal strings from different systems do not establish identity.

### C-034-2 — Attach key roles to exact fields

Record candidate key, composite key, foreign key or display label only with documentation or scoped sample evidence. Separate observed uniqueness from full-release uniqueness.

**Verify:** A sample unique identifier is not certified globally; composite facility/report/year keys remain composite.

### C-034-3 — Validate identity and historical scope

Add retired/reused IDs, changed geography vintage and multiple identifiers per organization to fixtures. Keep human-reviewed resolution decisions versioned.

**Verify:** An identifier match outside its applicability period becomes ambiguous or incompatible, not exact.
